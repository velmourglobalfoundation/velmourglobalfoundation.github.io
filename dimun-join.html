Join as an Intern or Volunteer

Intern — Interns form the core working body of Velmour Global Foundation. It is a hands-on role that involves daily, dedicated work across our ongoing projects and initiatives. Open to candidates who have passed Grade 10 (10th) or above.

Volunteer — Volunteers are individuals who share our vision and ideology and extend their support to the Foundation on an occasional basis. Volunteers are called upon as and when required, particularly for events, drives and outreach activities.
