About DIMUN

A youth-led platform for diplomacy, leadership and learning.

DIMUN is a youth-led initiative established in 2024 to create meaningful opportunities for young people to engage with diplomacy, international affairs, leadership, public discourse and community life. Through Model United Nations conferences, orientations, fests, educational activities and community initiatives, DIMUN seeks to develop informed, confident and socially responsible young people.

Operating under the aegis of Velmour Global Foundation

Model United Nations, international affairs, negotiation and debate.

Cleanliness drives and civic / community initiatives.

Our Activities

More Than a Conference Organiser

01

Model United Nations

The flagship activity — students participate as delegates in simulations of international organisations, political institutions and specialised committees, focused on diplomacy, research, negotiation and debate.

02

MUN Orientations

Large-scale orientation programmes introducing students to what MUN is, diplomacy and international affairs, committee procedure, public speaking and research. A single orientation can reach 2,500+ students.

03

Youth & Cultural Fests

Student-focused fests and cultural events creating opportunities for young people to participate beyond formal MUN environments — cultural, creative and academic engagement.

04

Cleanliness & Community Drives

Cleanliness drives and community-oriented activities reflecting DIMUN's focus on civic responsibility and encouraging students to contribute positively to their communities.
