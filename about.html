Who We Are

Velmour Global Foundation

Velmour Global Foundation is a non-profit organisation established to promote youth empowerment, education, leadership development, employment opportunities, healthcare initiatives, humanitarian assistance, and social welfare activities at regional, national, and international levels.

The organisation functions through its Directors, an Advisory Council of experienced professionals, and a dedicated team of youth members and volunteers working towards the fulfilment of its objectives — starting in Jammu & Kashmir and reaching outward, nationally and internationally.

Our Governance

How We Are Structured

Directors

Hold equal authority and decision-making power over the Foundation's administrative, operational and strategic direction, in mutual consultation.

Advisory Council

Experienced professionals from education, international relations, healthcare, entrepreneurship, law and technology, providing guidance in an advisory capacity.

Youth Team & Volunteers

Selected on skill, dedication and commitment — implementing programmes, initiatives and campaigns on the ground.

Our Objectives

What We Work Towards

01

Global Awareness & Leadership Education

Educational conferences, simulations, workshops and interactive forums that build leadership, critical thinking, communication and public speaking among students and youth.

02

Community Employment & Livelihood Development

Sustainable employment through skill development, vocational training, mentorship and professional guidance — encouraging entrepreneurship and self-reliance.

03

Global Medical Outreach & Professional Exchange

Healthcare outreach programmes, humanitarian medical assistance to underserved communities, and knowledge exchange among healthcare workers.

04

Regional & International Philanthropy

Welfare projects beginning in Jammu & Kashmir and expanding towards national and international humanitarian efforts.

Our Initiatives

DIMUN is a Velmour Initiative

DIMUN — Diplomacy Initiatives Model United Nations — is a youth-led Model United Nations and student engagement initiative operating under the aegis of Velmour Global Foundation. Where Velmour sets the broader humanitarian and leadership framework, DIMUN channels that mission specifically into diplomacy, debate and youth civic engagement.
