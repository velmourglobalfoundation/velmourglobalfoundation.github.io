Who We Are

A foundation built on youth, leadership and service.

Velmour Global Foundation is a non-profit organisation established to promote youth empowerment, education, leadership development, employment opportunities, healthcare initiatives, humanitarian assistance and social welfare — at regional, national and international levels.

We work through our Directors, an Advisory Council of experienced professionals, and a growing team of youth members and volunteers — turning ideas into initiatives that begin in Jammu & Kashmir and extend outward.

Global Awareness & Leadership Education

Conferences, simulations and workshops that build critical thinking, communication and public speaking.

Community Employment & Livelihood

Skill development, vocational training, mentorship and entrepreneurship support for youth.

Global Medical Outreach

Healthcare outreach, humanitarian medical assistance and professional exchange.

Regional & International Philanthropy

Welfare projects beginning in Jammu & Kashmir, expanding nationally and internationally.

DIMUN — Diplomacy Initiatives
Model United Nations

A youth-led Model United Nations and student engagement initiative based in Jammu & Kashmir, established in 2024. DIMUN develops diplomacy, leadership and civic engagement in young people through conferences, orientations, fests and community drives.
